# Literature verification and scope

The research implementation draws on the *topic* established in the earlier literature-review assignment, namely accuracy of selecting tools in multi-tool agentic AI. The original review PDF was subsequently provided and inspected directly (13 pages, September 2026). Its sections 3–9 distinguish tool identification, necessity, arguments, execution, statefulness, robustness and cost; the revised review in this repository is editorially based on that source.

The following original publications or official project sites were consulted on 14 November 2026:

1. Patil et al. (2023), *Gorilla: Large Language Model Connected with Massive APIs*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15334 — APIBench and retrieval-augmented API use.
2. Qin et al. (2024), *ToolLLM*, ICLR, https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/28e50ee5b72e90b50e7196fde8ea260e-Abstract-Conference.html — large-scale tool corpus and API retrieval.
3. Lu et al. (2025), *ToolSandbox*, NAACL Findings, https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-naacl.65/ — stateful tool use and dependency evaluation.
4. Faghih et al. (2025), *Tool Preferences in Agentic LLMs are Unreliable*, EMNLP, https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1060/ — selection fragility under description edits.
5. Shi et al. (2025), *Retrieval Models Aren't Tool-Savvy*, ACL Findings, https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1258/ — tool retrieval evaluation.
6. Liu et al. (2026), *ToolScope*, ACL, https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1573/ — redundant descriptions and context-aware retrieval.
7. Blankenstein et al. (2026), *BiasBusters*, ICLR, https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/a79875cc0d046ce7ce65f03f3affaa9e-Abstract-Conference.html — equivalent tools and metadata bias.
8. Yao et al. (2024), *tau-bench*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045 — agent reliability through user/tool interactions.

**Interpretation boundary:** These publications motivate the research question. This project does not reproduce any of their reported results or assert direct metric comparability. Its claimed contribution is solely an inspectable controlled pilot and analysis of matched selectors and constraint ablations.
